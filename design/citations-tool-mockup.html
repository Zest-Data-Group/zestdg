Where to find these numbers on Google Scholar
  1. Open your profile. The Cited by bar chart on the right shows citations per year; hover each bar to read it. Those go in All papers.
  2. Click a paper's title. Its page has the same chart for that paper alone; hover the bars and enter them in that paper's column.
  3. Tick to date on the current year so it is drawn hatched.

Your numbers are saved in this browser so they are here next time. The share link packs them into the address, so no account and no server. The chart script is open source.
